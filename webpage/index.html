Main findings

  • 62.8% of all expeditions reached the summit, and weather or snow conditions stop climbers more often than accidents.
  • Oxygen decides the 8000 m peaks: on Everest 91.4% succeeded with oxygen against 1.8% without it.
  • 2015 was the weakest year (23.9%) because of the Nepal earthquake, and success stayed near 70–76% afterwards.
  • Annapurna I had the highest death rate, 18.5 per 1000 climbers.
  • Large teams (10 or more members) succeeded in 77.6% of cases, against 56.4% for teams of up to 3.
Data: Himalayan Database (Tidy Tuesday). Analysis done in Python with Pandas and NumPy, charts drawn with Chart.js.
